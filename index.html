Who We Are

Your Trusted Technology Partner in Malaysia

Omegatec Computers Sdn. Bhd. was established on August 3, 2001 with a firm commitment to delivering innovative, reliable, and high-performance IT solutions. Based in George Town, Penang, we have grown into a cornerstone technology partner for government agencies, universities, and commercial enterprises.

Authorised with official BUMIPUTERA status and registered with KEMENTERIAN KEWANGAN MALAYSIA (Ministry of Finance Malaysia), Omegatec Computers provides end-to-end computer-related goods and services with an unwavering pursuit of Total Customer Satisfaction.
