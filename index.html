About Us

Omegatec Computers Sdn. Bhd. was established on August 3, 2001, and is based in Malaysia. Our areas of expertise include Computer sales, services, networking & fiber optic solutions, system and app development, website designing, IT consulting; cloud computing services; cybersecurity solutions; infrastructure and database management; artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) solutions; and other IT-enabled services. The Company shall also provide consultancy, advisory, and training services in the areas of Information Technology. The company has registered with KEMENTERIAN KEWANGAN MALAYSIA (Ministry of Finance Malaysia), we are committed to delivering innovative IT solutions.
